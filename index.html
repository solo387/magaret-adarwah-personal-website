Good to know

Before we talk.

A few helpful notes about getting started.

What should I bring to a first conversation?

Bring any questions you have. If you want to review existing coverage, having the policy documents available can help—but you can start with a conversation, too.

Am I expected to buy something?

No. The initial conversation is an opportunity to discuss what matters to you. If you’re already adequately covered, that can be the answer.

Can we talk remotely?

Remote conversations may be available where licensing allows. Reach out to discuss your location and what you’re looking for.
